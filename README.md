# Environmental Justice in New Mexico
## EDS 223 Homework Assignment 1 



![Source: [Oil & Gas Threat Map](https://oilandgasthreatmap.com/public-lands/new-mexico/)](https://oilandgasthreatmap.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/04/newmexico-publiclands-500x500.png){width=300px}


### Why New Mexico?


New Mexico, also known the Land of Enchantment, has a rich cultural history developed from a combination of Native American and Hispanic Heritage. Notably, it has 23 recognized Native American Nations, 4 Native reservations, and It has the largest proportion of Hispanic population in the nation ([World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/state-rankings/hispanic-population-by-state), [New Mexico Route 66 Association](https://www.rt66nm.org/native-american-communities.html)).

However, New Mexico faces major enviornmental justice concerns. In 2025, the state outputed 17% of the U.S. crude oil production and 9% of the U.S. natural gas withdrawals, making the country's second largest crude oil producer and the third largest natural gas producer. 
([U.S. Energy Information Administration](https://www.eia.gov/states/NM/overview)). Due to the intense infrastructure of fossil fuel extraction, it estimated that over 144,000 New Mexicans live within a half mile threat radius of active oil and gas operations ([Rio Grande Sierra Club](https://www.riograndesierraclub.org/new-mexicans-threaened-by-oil-pollution/)). 


### Purpose

This repository maps the mean percentile of toxic air releases and the mean percentile of people of color to analyze how the environmental burden of fossil fuel extraction may impact marginzalized communities in New Mexico counties.
